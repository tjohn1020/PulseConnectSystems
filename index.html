Nurse Call

PulseConnect specializes in Austco Tacera nurse call systems — full-scale configuration, commissioning, and ongoing support for hospitals and rehabilitation facilities. Our technical scope includes:

  • Tacera server builds and software configuration (Proxmox virtualization)
  • RTLS deployment and HL7/ADT integration with EMR systems
  • New construction installs and system conversions from legacy platforms
  • Structured cabling and infrastructure support for nurse call networks
  • Ongoing system upgrades, troubleshooting, and remote programming support

We're the integrator behind Tacera installations for a national rehabilitation healthcare system, with active projects across multiple states. Whether it's a new-construction build or converting a facility off an outdated system, we bring hands-on Tacera expertise from first commissioning to long-term support.

Cameras

PulseConnect designs and installs video surveillance systems for healthcare facilities, commercial buildings, and multi-tenant properties. Drawing on our structured cabling and low-voltage infrastructure background, we deliver camera systems that integrate cleanly with your facility's existing network and security infrastructure. Our services include:

  • IP camera system design and installation (PoE-based), including Hanwha Vision, Axis, and Ubiquiti UniFi Protect surveillance systems
  • Structured cabling and network infrastructure to support surveillance systems
  • Integration with existing access control, intercom, and security networks
  • System configuration and remote monitoring setup

We bring the same low-voltage infrastructure expertise from our nurse call and access control work to every camera installation, ensuring your surveillance system is built on a solid, reliable foundation.

Intercom & Access Control

PulseConnect brings over a decade of low-voltage experience in intercom and access control systems, spanning healthcare facilities, commercial buildings, and multi-tenant properties. Our background includes structured cabling, security system integration, and commercial AV platforms (QSYS, Crestron), giving us the technical depth to design and install systems that work reliably alongside your existing infrastructure. Our services include:

  • Video and audio intercom system design and installation, including 2N IP intercom systems
  • Access control system integration — card readers, keypads, and smart lock systems, including Ubiquiti UniFi Access
  • Structured cabling and low-voltage infrastructure to support secure entry systems
  • Integration with existing nurse call, camera, and security networks for unified facility communication

Whether you're securing a healthcare facility, office building, or multi-unit property, we design access control and intercom systems that fit your specific security and workflow needs.
